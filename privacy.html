2. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the Cuoco app, why we collect it, how we use it, and what rights you have over it.

Cuoco is an AI-powered recipe management app that allows you to import, organise, and cook recipes using your voice. To provide these features, we work with a number of third-party services, each described below.

3. Data We Collect and Why

3.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used to identify your account, communicate with you about your subscription, and provide account recovery. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, Apple provides a private relay address instead of your real address. We store whichever address Apple or Google provides and use it solely for account management purposes.
  • User ID — a unique identifier assigned by our authentication provider (Supabase) to link your data securely.
  • Name and profile picture link — if you sign in with Google, we receive your name and a link to your profile picture, where your Google account provides them. We use these only to show your profile in Settings. We do not receive these from Sign In with Apple.

Legal basis (GDPR): Contract — necessary to provide the service you have signed up for.

3.2 Recipe Data

Recipes you import, create, or edit are stored securely in our database. This includes recipe titles, ingredients, steps, nutritional information, notes, and any metadata (cuisine type, meal type, dietary flags). This data belongs to you and is used solely to power your Cuoco experience.

Legal basis (GDPR): Contract — necessary to provide core app functionality.

3.3 Usage and Billing Data

To manage your subscription tier and enforce fair usage limits, we collect:

  • Your current subscription tier (Trial, Cuoco Pro, Cuoco Premium, or Admin)
  • Monthly AI usage costs and counts (recipe imports, voice assistant calls, cook mode sessions)
  • Trial start and end dates
  • Subscription expiry dates

This data is used to determine what features you can access and to display your usage in the app's Settings screen. It is not shared with third parties except as necessary to process your subscription (see Section 5).

Legal basis (GDPR): Contract — necessary to enforce the subscription terms you have agreed to.

3.4 Device Information

When you use Cuoco, we collect limited device information to help us understand how the app is being used and to detect misuse:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • A device identifier that Apple provides for our app (this is not an advertising identifier)
  • Approximate country and city, worked out from your IP address when the app connects. To do this, your IP address is sent to IPinfo (see Section 5). We store only a hashed version of your IP address and do not keep it in readable form

Legal basis (GDPR): Legitimate interests — to maintain the security and integrity of our service and prevent abuse of the free trial.

3.5 Voice and AI Interactions

Recipe import (from a photo, link, or pasted text) and the voice assistant are optional AI-powered features, turned off by default. Before you can use them, we explain what data is sent and to whom, and ask for your explicit permission — either during onboarding or the first time you try to use one of these features. You can turn this on or off at any time in Settings.

When these features are enabled and you use them, your input is processed by our AI providers (see Section 5). Specifically:

  • Voice recordings are sent to OpenAI to be transcribed, and are then discarded — we do not store audio files
  • When you give a voice command that needs confirming, the microphone reopens for a few seconds so you can answer "yes" or "no" out loud. It closes on its own, only ever follows a command you have just given, and you can answer using the on-screen buttons instead
  • Recipe text, URLs, and photos submitted for import are sent to OpenAI for processing and are not retained by us beyond what is returned as a recipe
  • Cook Mode and voice assistant queries are sent to OpenAI for processing; spoken responses are sent to ElevenLabs to generate speech. Within a single Cook Mode session, your recent questions and the assistant's answers are kept in memory and included with follow-up questions so it can understand them in context; this history is never stored, and is cleared when you end the session or close the app
  • Shopping list item names you add — whether typed manually or added by voice — along with your current shopping list, are sent to OpenAI to group similar items together and sort them into categories
  • Wake word ("Hey Cuoco"). If you turn this on, Cuoco listens for the phrase "Hey Cuoco" while Cook Mode is open. To recognise that phrase, your iPhone converts nearby speech into text using Apple's built-in on-device speech recognition. This happens entirely on your iPhone. No audio is recorded, saved, or sent to us or to OpenAI, and the text produced is discarded moment by moment as you speak — it is not stored and it never leaves your device. Only when you actually say "Hey Cuoco" is the question that follows sent to OpenAI to be answered, exactly as when you press the microphone button. The feature is off unless you switch it on, works only while Cook Mode is open, and stops the moment you leave that screen or switch to another app. When you turn it on, iOS shows Apple's standard speech recognition notice, which appears for every app that uses speech recognition.

Legal basis (GDPR): Consent — these features only operate once you have given explicit permission, which you may withdraw at any time via Settings.

3.6 Error Logs

When an AI feature fails (e.g. a recipe import error), we log structured error information including the endpoint, error code, and response time. This helps us diagnose and fix problems. Logs do not contain the content of your request.

Legal basis (GDPR): Legitimate interests — to maintain and improve the reliability of our service.

3.7 Connected AI Assistants

You can choose to connect an AI assistant that supports connectors — such as Claude (by Anthropic) or ChatGPT (by OpenAI) — to your Cuoco account, so you can ask it about, and have it help manage, your recipes, collections, meal plan and shopping list. Nothing is shared until you set this up yourself: you sign in to Cuoco, we show you which assistant is asking for access and what it will be able to do, and it only gets access if you choose Allow.

Once connected, and when you ask it to, the assistant can:

  • Search and read your saved recipes, including ingredients, steps and notes
  • Edit a saved recipe — for example its title, notes, rating, ingredients or method. Edits made this way cannot be undone
  • See your collections and favourites, create or rename a collection, add or remove recipes from a collection, and mark a recipe as a favourite
  • See which recipes you have cooked recently and most often
  • See your meal plan, and add or remove entries one day at a time
  • See your shopping list, add items to it, tick items off, edit or remove an item, and clear the items you have ticked
  • Save a new recipe to your library from a web link or from text in your conversation, and sort items you add to your shopping list into categories — these use Cuoco's AI features as described in Section 3.5, and only work if you have turned them on
  • Read your basic Cuoco settings, such as your preferred units and plan type

It cannot delete recipes or collections, clear your whole calendar or shopping list in one go, or change your account or subscription.

Information the assistant reads from Cuoco is then handled by that assistant's provider under their own privacy policy, not ours — we recommend reading it. You can disconnect an assistant at any time in Settings → Connected Apps, which ends its access immediately.

Legal basis (GDPR): Consent — data is shared with an assistant only after you explicitly allow it, and you may withdraw that consent at any time by disconnecting it.

3.8 Android Waitlist

If you join the Android waitlist on our website, we store the email address you enter and the date you signed up. We use it only to email you when Cuoco is available on Android. It is not linked to a Cuoco account, and we do not use it for anything else. When you sign up, we also receive a notification email containing your address, sent through Resend (see Section 5).

Legal basis (GDPR): Consent — you give us your email address for this purpose. You can ask us to remove it at any time by emailing hello@get-cuoco.app.

We do not collect:

6. Subscriptions and Payments

All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, store, or process your payment card details at any point.

When you purchase a subscription, Apple notifies us via RevenueCat of the subscription status (active, cancelled, expired, etc.). We use this information to unlock the appropriate features in Cuoco.

If you cancel your subscription, your access continues until the end of the paid period. After that point, your account reverts to an expired trial state and you will be asked to resubscribe to continue using AI features.

9. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — you can request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — you can ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — you can ask us to delete your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Right to restriction — you can ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — you can ask for your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object — you can object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Rights related to automated decision-making — Cuoco does not make solely automated decisions that have legal or significant effects on you

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@get-cuoco.app. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you believe we have not handled your data correctly.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app: go to Settings and choose Delete Account. This takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, so please cancel it in the App Store first.

If you cannot use the app, you can instead email hello@get-cuoco.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and the email address associated with your account. We will process emailed requests within 30 days.

Section 7 explains the records we keep after deletion.
